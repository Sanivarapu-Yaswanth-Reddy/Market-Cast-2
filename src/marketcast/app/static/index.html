Holdings

Simulated range of each holding over the horizon and its share of the portfolio's worst-5% loss. Largest 60 shown.

Market trend odds

Share of scenarios in which the market ends more than half a typical move down, within it, or above it.

Sectors most exposed

Chance that a sector is among the three worst performers relative to the market over the horizon. Sectors with only a few member companies swing more, so they tend to rank high here and among the best three as well.

Scores so far

Mean over every date scored to date. CRPS and QLIKE: lower is better. “Inside 90%” should be near 90%; “below 5% level” near 5%. A model is only comparable with a baseline on the same split and folds.
